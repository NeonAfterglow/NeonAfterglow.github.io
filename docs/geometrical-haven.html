Geometrical Haven

● IN TESTING ANDROID FREE ARCADE

Bored? Race the clock through glowing neon levels in a one-tap speed-run game that's easy to pick up and hard to master. Find the fastest route to the finish, then come back and do it faster.

How to play it Features
Screenshots
Shapes that move you

Every shape is a move

🔺

Triangles

Snap in, then tap to launch toward your finger.

⭕

Circles

Get caught in orbit, tap to reverse, fly off with a speed boost.

〰️

Rails

Ride them, build speed, tap to swap sides and dodge hazards.

⏩

Boosts and walls

Boost pads fire you forward. Mirror, kicker and bumper walls bounce you around.

🟣

Sludge

Slows you right down. Find a way around it.

🔴

Hazards

Red means dead. One touch and you restart.

What's inside

Built for speed

  • 10 handcrafted levels, each with more than one path
  • Bronze, silver and gold medal times to beat
  • 27 achievements, each paying bonus coins
  • Skins and themes: glowing balls with their own trails and sounds, and themes that restyle every level
  • Quick sessions: short levels, instant restarts, plays offline
