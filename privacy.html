Geometrical Haven: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Geometrical Haven ("the game"), made by Chase Pelechytik ("we") under the name Neon Afterglow, collects and how it is used. It applies to the game on Android (Google Play) and iOS (the App Store).

Information the game stores on your device

Your progress (unlocked levels, best times, medals, coins, items you own and equip), your settings (volumes, vibration) and your play time are saved only on your device. We do not have accounts, and this data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game, or using Settings > Reset All Data, deletes it.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: occasional full-screen ads, and optional ads you can choose to watch for in-game coins. To show and measure ads, Google and its ad partners may collect and use information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (on Android, the Google advertising ID; on iPhone and iPad, Apple's IDFA, only if you allow tracking, see below),
  • your IP address and approximate location (derived from it),
  • device information (model, operating system, language),
  • how you interact with ads (views, taps).

This information is collected by Google, not by us. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent: the first time you open the game, it asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can accept, refuse ("Do not consent"), or choose in detail ("Manage options"). If you refuse, you may still see ads, but they won't be personalized. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options.
  • iPhone and iPad (App Tracking Transparency): the game asks for your permission before your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) can be used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared and you may still see ads, but they won't be based on that tracking. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • US states with privacy laws (such as California): you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information in Settings > Privacy options.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your phone's settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads; iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).
  • Ads you choose to watch for coins are always optional.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (under 14 in Quebec, or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Person in charge of personal information

Contact him with any privacy question, complaint, or request to access, correct or delete information. Because the game keeps your progress only on your device, most of that information can be deleted directly with Settings > Reset All Data or by uninstalling the game; for information held by Google, see Google's Privacy Policy linked above.
