FAQ

Quick answers

Is Geometrical Haven on iPhone?

Not yet. It's Android-first for now.

Is it free?

Yes. It's free to play with ads, and optional ads give you bonus coins.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored on your phone. Use Settings > Reset All Data in the game, or uninstall it. See Delete your data for the details.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. Ads just won't show without an internet connection.
