Geometrical Haven: Delete Your Data

Developer: Chase Pelechytik · Last updated: November 22, 2026

Geometrical Haven has no accounts. Your progress stays on your phone, and the only data kept online is your leaderboard entry (a random player ID, your nickname and your best times). Here is how to delete everything connected to the game.

1. Delete your game data (on your device)

Your progress (levels, best times, medals, coins, skins, themes, achievements) and your settings are saved only on your phone. To delete them, do either of these:

  • In the game, open Settings > Reset All Data and tap it twice. Everything is erased immediately.
  • Uninstall the game. Android, iOS and iPadOS delete all of its saved data.

Nothing is kept afterwards on your device, so deleted progress can't be restored. This does not remove your leaderboard entries, which are stored online (see step 2).

2. Delete your leaderboard data (online)

From version 1.1, if you've played while connected to the internet, the online leaderboards (run for us by Talo) hold a random player ID, your leaderboard nickname, your best time on each level, and your best time on each weekly challenge you played. To have all of it deleted:

  1. Find your nickname: in the game, open a level, tap LEADERBOARD, and look for Name: near the top.
  2. Email neonafterglow06@gmail.com with the subject "Geometrical Haven: delete my leaderboard data" and your nickname. If you can, add one of your best times so we can find the right player.

We delete your player record, nickname and all your times from every leaderboard within 30 days and reply to confirm. Nothing is kept afterwards. If you play online again later, the game will put your new best times back on the boards.

3. Advertising data (Google AdMob)

Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), device information and ad interactions. This data is held by Google, not by us. To control it:

  • Delete or reset your advertising ID: on your Android device, open Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads) and choose Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.
  • On iPhone or iPad: open Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and turn tracking off for Geometrical Haven (or for all apps).
  • Change your ad consent: in the game, open Settings > Privacy options.
  • Google's data: see How Google uses information from apps that use its services and manage it at myadcenter.google.com.

4. Questions or requests

If you have any question about your data, email neonafterglow06@gmail.com with the subject "Geometrical Haven data". We reply within 30 days.
