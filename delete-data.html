Geometrical Haven: Delete Your Data

Developer: Chase Pelechytik · Last updated: November 18, 2026

Geometrical Haven has no accounts, and we (the developer) do not receive or store any of your personal data on our servers. Here is how to delete everything connected to the game.

1. Delete your game data (on your device)

Your progress (levels, best times, medals, coins, skins, themes, achievements) and your settings are saved only on your phone. To delete them, do either of these:

  • In the game, open Settings > Reset All Data and tap it twice. Everything is erased immediately.
  • Uninstall the game. Android deletes all of its saved data.

Nothing is kept afterwards: there is no copy anywhere else, so deleted progress can't be restored.

2. Advertising data (Google AdMob)

Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), device information and ad interactions. This data is held by Google, not by us. To control it:

  • Delete or reset your advertising ID: on your Android device, open Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads) and choose Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.
  • Change your ad consent: in the game, open Settings > Privacy options.
  • Google's data: see How Google uses information from apps that use its services and manage it at myadcenter.google.com.

3. Questions or requests

If you have any question about your data, email Chasepelechytik1@gmail.com with the subject "Geometrical Haven data". We reply within 30 days.
